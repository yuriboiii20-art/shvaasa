Meet Dr. Swathi N

“Our core philosophy is informed decision-making. We believe every patient deserves to clearly understand the root cause of their symptoms before any treatment or surgical procedure is initiated.”

Dr. Swathi N is an ENT Surgeon, Head & Neck specialist, and certified Allergist & Immunologist based in Bangalore. With over 15 years of comprehensive medical experience (12 years as a specialist), she specializes in the diagnosis and management of complex ENT disorders, chronic allergic rhinitis, asthma, and reactive airway diseases.
